Each counterexample below has one shape.  A send raises the pending count; the other domain then reads that count, or
sends and takes a stamp; only then does the first send take its own stamp.  No order of atomic sends gives that history.
The cause is assumed, not tested.  E4_sched.enqueue (ocaml/engine/e4_sched.ml) enters the quiescence count and takes the
stamp inside its own mailbox's lock; `pending` and `last_seq` read without that lock, and two sends to two machines hold
two different locks.
