### The bugs

**K1, two writers.** `E4_pack.acquire_lock` (`ocaml/engine/cas/e4_pack.ml`) checks and fills the
in-process registry `held`, a plain `Hashtbl`, with no lock. `Unix.lockf` does not refuse a
second descriptor of one process, so two domains pass both guards.

- tested (`race_open.log`): `E4_pack.open_` from two domains at once gave two writers in 1,871
  of 2,000 trials on a fresh directory. With the pack directory made first, it gave two in 1,938.
  In 26 trials a writer raised `Bad_pack` on a segment head the other was still writing.
- tested (`race_disk.log`): `E4_disk.open_` from two domains passed the lock twice in 291 of 300
  trials. In 274 of them one open then failed on the shared `control.tmp`, and 17 kept two live
  writers. 9 trials were refused with `Locked`, as PK6 states.
- tested: K1 in `stm_disk.ml` finds `Open 0 ‖ Open 1`, both opened.
- reading: `E4_wal`'s `claims` table (`ocaml/engine/cas/e4_wal.ml`, LG7) has the same shape and
  was not run.

**K2, a refused opener breaks the holder.** `E4_cas.open_rw_exn` (`ocaml/engine/cas/e4_cas.ml`)
runs `E4_control.cleanup`, which removes `control.tmp`, before `E4_pack.open_at` takes the lock.
A second opener is refused with `Locked`, as CS4 states, but first it removes the live writer's
temporary file. The writer's rename fails, and `E4_disk.commit` closes the writer.

- tested (`race_commit.log`): alone, the holder committed 200 of 200 advances. With a second
  domain opening, its first commit failed with `rename … control.tmp: No such file or
  directory`, and none of its 200 commits landed.
- reading: `E4_disk.commit` closes a writer whose commit faults, so the later commits fail on a
  closed writer. The opener then gets a writer of its own, as the log shows once.
- tested (`race_process.log`): with a forked process opening instead, refused with `Locked`
  59,007 times, the parent's first commit failed the same way.
- tested: K2 in `stm_disk.ml` finds `Advance 0` refused on `control.tmp` beside `Open 1` locked.
- reading: the same function reads the control file before the lock. Suppose an opener stalls
  between that read and the lock while another writer commits and closes. It would then write
  the stale roots back with the new pack end. Not run.
