## Breaker register (open findings, newest first; each measured against the algebraic model of the core)

The model the findings are measured against: `Eff` is the initial algebra of the program signature over `Term`; `effTy` is the algebra homomorphism into `Option EffTy` and `HasTy` its graph (sound and complete); `Ty` modulo `normalize` is the type object and `CTy` its section; the reference is the free monad over `RSig` read by address in one root; the machine is the frame coalgebra over points into that root; printer and reader are a partial section-retraction pair between `Eff` and the pinned syntax; `Forms` is a template algebra with `expand` its homomorphism; hoisting is a path lens (`layerAt`/`replaceLayerAt` with PutGet and GetPut, `replaceLayerAt_spec`).

- **B19, no inverse for types: agreement is checked by projection into the target language (10:05).** With `ofTy : Ty → Option TypeRef` and the carrier's decidable equality (`TypeRef.beq_iff`) in the tree, the reader's declaration check is `ofTy ty.answer = some a ∧ ofTy ty.error = some e ∧ declared = Effect.Effect<a, e>` on the principal type `typeOfProgram` computes; no `readTy`, no `AdmittedTy`, no `readTy_printTy`. The collisions that made O2 hard (`nat` and `int` both `number`, `unit` as `void`, handle text) are harmless because nothing inverts: the certificate's type is the core's view, the declaration is the host's view, and `ofTy` is the view map. Algebraically the surface needs the projection to be a function on `Ty` modulo `normalize` (`ofTy_normalize`, landed), never a section. The same principle settles the two other places a type meets source: binder annotations under the annotated profile (the printer annotates with `ofTy` of the binder's type from the certificate's `TyEnv`, and exactness is the computed field `print p = ok x`, so there is no annotation-checking pass and no eighth copy of the binder discipline of B17), and the S3c service shape (rows through `ofTy`, compared structurally). Decision raised: retire `readTy`, `AdmittedTy`, D1 and O2 as first stated; O2′ is `ofTy` canonical and normalize-respecting plus the reader's declaration check; `TypedModule.admittedTy` becomes `representable`.
