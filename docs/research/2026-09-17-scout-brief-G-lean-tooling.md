## The work the tooling is for

The estate (`/Users/pooks/Dev/lean4-effect4`, Lean `v4.33.1`, packages in `lake-manifest.json`:
batteries, aesop, effects, typescript, hash) is being made coherent under one principle: each
kind of thing has one free object (an inductive family with its signature as data — `Eff`,
`Ty`, `Term`, `Representation`, `Store.Val`), every other representation is an algebra of it
reached by the unique fold (`cataFam`, `cata_ty`, … in `src/Effect4/Program/Fold.lean`,
generated by `tools/Effect4Gen/Fold.lean`) or an exact embedding, and coherence is checked by a
census of algebras. The first measurement just landed
(`src/Effect4/Laws/Auto/Traversals.lean`, `#traversal_census`): of 499 definitions that take an
`Eff`-family value, 22 go through a declared fold and **157 are hand `match`/structural
recursions**; `Ty` 26, `Term` 34, `Representation` 17, `Store.Val` 48 (no fold declared at all).
The next task is to *convert* those hand traversals into algebras mechanically — a
metaprogram that reads a structural definition's equations and emits the algebra record plus an
agreement theorem — and to keep the census as a gate. In parallel: TypeScript generation from
tables and from Lean's LCNF (`src/OCaml5/Lcnf/*`), deriving handlers for `Canonical`
instances (`tools/Effect4Gen/Main.lean`), and proof automation (aesop rule sets are in
`src/Effect4/Laws/Auto/Inversion.lean`).

## The question

What exists, today, in the Lean 4 ecosystem that would do part of this for us or make it much
cheaper, at Lean 4.33-compatible versions? Concretely, for each of these needs, find the
candidates, say what each actually does (read its source or docs, not its README's promise),
whether it builds on 4.33 (check its `lean-toolchain` and last commit), and how it would slot in:

1. **Environment census and linting.** Walking the environment, classifying definitions,
   reporting: Mathlib's linter framework (`Mathlib/Tactic/Linter/*`, `Batteries.Tactic.Lint`),
   `#find`/`#help`, `Lean.Linter` infrastructure, `lean4checker`, `LeanInk`, `doc-gen4`'s
   analyzer, `import-graph`. Which of these could replace or extend `#traversal_census`
   (`src/Effect4/Laws/Auto/Traversals.lean`, 220 lines) and the axiom gate
   (`Test/Audit/AxiomGate.lean`)?
2. **Recursion schemes and generic programming over inductives.** Anything that generates
   catamorphisms / algebras / functor instances / traversals from an inductive declaration
   (deriving handlers for `Functor`/`Traversable`/`Foldable`, "recursion schemes" libraries,
   Mathlib's `Fix`/`QPF`/`MvQPF` machinery, `deriving` frameworks like
   `Lean.Elab.Deriving.*` reusable pieces, any third-party generic-programming library). Which
   could generate `EffAlgebra`/`cataFam` from the signature, or better, generate the *conversion*
   of a structural definition into an algebra?
3. **Refactoring by metaprogram.** Tools that rewrite definitions across a project: Mathlib's
   `to_additive` transport, `Mathlib.Tactic.Translate`, rename/deprecation infrastructure
   (`@[deprecated]` with `since`), `lean4-refactor`-style tools, `Lean.Server` code actions,
   `leanprover-community/repl`, `lake exe` scripts that edit sources. What would let us convert
   157 hand traversals with a metaprogram rather than by hand?
4. **Derive handlers and codegen.** Third-party deriving handlers (`DeriveHandler`s for
   `ToJson`/`FromJson`/`Repr`/`Ord`/`Hashable` are Lean's; others: `lean4-derive`,
   Mathlib `Deriving`), and code-generation frameworks that emit Lean source from tables
   (`Verso`? `lean4-alloy`? `Lean.Elab.Command` quoting patterns). What is state of the art for
   "write Lean declarations from data" so `tools/Effect4Gen` can be smaller?
5. **Extraction and lowering.** Lean 4 → other languages beyond the C backend: `lean4-wasm`,
   `lean2js`, LCNF consumers (`Lean.Compiler.LCNF` users outside the compiler), `Lean.IR`
   dumpers, anything reading `.olean` mono LCNF as we do (`OCaml5.Lcnf`), the `lean4export`
   tool. What could the TypeScript lowering reuse?
6. **Proof automation beyond aesop.** `omega`/`grind`/`decide +kernel` are in the toolchain;
   `duper`, `egg`, `lean-smt`, `LeanCopilot`, `Canonical`, `polyrith`, `bv_decide` — which are
   4.33-compatible and would help the machine proofs (structural, over inductives with many
   constructors; see `src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Agreement/Machine.lean`)?
7. **Testing.** `plausible` (slim_check's successor), `LSpec`, `lean4-fuzz`: for the conform
   rungs (`tools/Conform/**`, 20,387 hand-generated vectors today).
8. **Docs and navigation.** `doc-gen4`, `Verso`, `LeanSearch`/`loogle` local instances,
   `lean-lsp` MCP (in use). Which reduce orientation cost for an agent in this estate?
