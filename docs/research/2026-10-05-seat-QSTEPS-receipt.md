## 7. Choices

1. **The names.** The module is in the namespace `Effect4.Queue`, and its laws in
   `Effect4.Queue.Model`, as the brief says. Three words of the probe changed: `ite` is `ifT`,
   `empty` is `isEmpty`, and `none_of` is `noneOf`. `Queue.empty` is the initial value.
2. **Every builder takes the message type first.** A builder that writes no declaration names
   it `_A` and does not read it. Only `empty` and `offerStep` read it.
3. **The capacity is a literal.** `Queue.empty A capacity` takes a `Nat`, so a caller states a
   positive capacity where it writes the queue.
4. **`takerTy`, `offerTy` and `cellTy` keep their literal field lists** (the coordinator's
   note). `empty_typed` and `sizeStep_typed` unfold the checker by `show` against the literal.
   The red control at a type that is not its normal form reads the difference between the two.
   The hand order is checked: `cellFields_normal` at every canonical type, and a guard at 27.
5. **The premise of a typing statement is `MessageTy A`.** It says that `A` is its own normal
   form, and that the cell's and an offer's declarations are formed. It is decidable.
6. **The typing statements stand at the step's own scope**, through `typeAt`. Another scope
   follows by `termTy_weaken`, which no statement of this slice applies.
7. **The step statements stand at every scope.** `Reads` says that a source term elaborates
   and that its tree has a value. `Captured` adds the same value under a fold's two binders.
   `captured_var` gives both for an author's variable.
8. **The table is injective on every identity**, and a step changes it in one way:
   `Table.renew`. The conclusion's table is a function of the reply: `Table.afterTake` and
   `Table.afterOffer`. So the replaced hint is written in the statement, and no control
   stands for it.
9. **`Notified` is a structure**: the signals are the answers of the offers that entered, then
   the wakes. Each entered offer was pending before the step, and each woken taker is stored
   after it.
10. **An offer's premises** are `Requested` and one more: no pending offer has the identity.
    The model's own `step` skips such an offer, and `offer` does not.
11. **The connector is `step_updates`**, stated once for the five steps of a `Ref.modify`. It
    takes a reading and uses `refStep_modify`. Its typed half is `step_keeps_cell`, through
    `ListFoldRules.step`.
12. **The read law for `size` is `refStep_get`** (addendum 1), as `cell_read`: a `Ref.get` of
    the cell answers its value and leaves the stores. `sizeStep` is a term over that value.
    `sizeStep_agrees` states the reply. No write was added to `size`.
13. **No module definition fixes a current-value name around a caller's term** (addendum 2).
    The module exports no row. Three batteries write `Ref.modify "s" …`, and each says why.
14. **The reading lemmas stay in the Queue's folder.** They name no Queue, and a second module
    would promote them to `src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Authoring/`. `Pointwise` is a local
    relation: this toolchain's core has no `List.Forall₂`.
15. **The workload is a plain battery**, as the dispatch says. Seat DOGFOOD's scenario record
    came into the branch by the merge `3d9d935c`, after the workload's design.
16. **The engine's lane compares the root's exit as the engine spells it.** The writer renders
    Lean's exit in that spelling, for Booleans, numbers and flat lists of them.
17. **The role register puts `src/Effect4/Modules` at height 4**, the lowest above `Program`.
    It shares that height with `Schema` and `Store/Domain`, which it does not import.
18. **`HandleIdentityLaws.freshDeferred` is not used here.** A step theorem takes freshness as
    a premise on model identities. The step from an allocation to that premise is the
    wrapper's.
