## 6. Landed theorems and their placement

**R12-b**, `frontier_empty_iff_deadlocked` (`src/Effect4/Laws/Api/Frontier.lean`):
`m.stuck = none → m.finished = false → (frontierReasons .tape m = [] ↔ Deadlocked m)`.
A machine is deadlocked (`Deadlocked`, the same file) when it is live and unfinished, and has
nothing to wait for. No fiber's current code is at its compile budget, and no fiber awaits a host
reply. No fiber sleeps on a timer or is runnable, and no owner is armed.

- Concept: `reactive-scheduling` (`docs/core/semantics.md` §2.4). Property: R12's
  "`Deadlocked` requires nothing armed". It is the frontier half of the classification that
  `scheduler-progress` asks for. The theorem carries `@[semantics "reactive-scheduling"]`,
  because its module's default concept is `host-session-protocol`.
- Question: the proposed registry claim `frontier-names-work`, role `inversion`, pointing at the
  theorem (section 9). No ledger goal held its place: the theorem landed with its proof.
  Consumers: the R12 requirement row, and the `scheduler-progress` classification once stated.
- Reach: the frontier observation `frontierReasons .tape` on every frame machine
  (`NativeMachine`), with no `Guard.Reachable` premise and no typing premise. Its two hypotheses
  are `m.stuck = none` and `m.finished = false`. Rows: 201 (b), DI-68 and row 139. Register:
  `E4-SCHED-CE-021`.
- Does not establish:
  - that the named decision steps the machine (progress; `scheduler-progress` stays absent);
  - what any decision does at a deadlocked machine;
  - liveness or fairness on infinite tapes (R12-c);
  - anything about the fuel site beyond `commandFuel_iff` (a fuel frontier always names
    `.commandFuel`, at a deadlock too);
  - INV-TAPE-2, which stays unruled: a decision reason names no row;
  - any change to `HostProtocol.observe`, which reads `parked` at armed work with no runnable
    fiber.
- Unlocks: R12's open part R12-b closes (section 9). It gives the classification of
  `scheduler-progress` (row 139) its frontier half. M7c leaves a frontier as the only unfinished
  end of an answer-free run, and R12-b says what that frontier names.

The scout's shape (`docs/research/2026-10-04-reference-scout/semantics.md` §3.4) quantified over
`Guard.Reachable` and stated one direction. The landed theorem drops that premise and states both
directions, so it implies the shape (stronger, not weaker). Its proof never reads reachability:
the classification is a property of the observation at any machine. The scout's `Deadlocked`
named the reason projections. This one names the machine's fields (`Program.awaits`, the timer
waiters, `isCompileFrontier`), in the frontier's order.

**The restated laws**, all in `src/Effect4/Laws/Api/Frontier.lean`, concept
`host-session-protocol` by module default, DI-68's laws as row 201 (b) amends them:

- `awaitDecision_iff`: `.awaitDecision ∈ frontierReasons why m ↔ why = .tape ∧ (hasRunnable m =
  true ∨ m.armed ≠ [])`. Consumers: `observe_idle_tape_iff`, `observe_of_reasons`, the truth
  harness. It does not establish that the decision steps the machine.
- `observe_idle_tape_iff`: with no host wait at a tape frontier,
  `(HostProtocol.observe m = .idle ∨ m.armed ≠ []) ↔ .awaitDecision ∈ frontierReasons .tape m`.
  The name is kept: it is DI-68's law slot. No consumer outside the module.
- `observe_of_reasons`: its fourth conjunct is `awaitDecision_iff`'s new statement. Consumer:
  `harness/truth/Truth.lean`, which uses the first conjunct only.
