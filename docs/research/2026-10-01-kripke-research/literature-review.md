# What “Kripke” means for Effect4

**Evidence:** source reading at implementation HEAD `26c7be34e6fea61fe2d13c4fa80d9bcd80d414d5`, 2026-10-01. No compiler, proof rerun, repository edits or downloads. Literature bodies were read from the vendored primary texts; Kripke 1963 and Simpson–Voorneveld 2019 were read online. The comparisons and proposed wording below are interpretations, not newly proved connectors.

## 1. Accessibility is an admissible extension relation, not the execution relation

Kripke's original modal semantics supplies worlds, an accessibility relation and a valuation; necessity evaluates a formula at every accessible world. His §2, printed p. 68, identifies reflexive/transitive accessibility with S4 model structures and gives the universal clause for necessity. This construction does not intrinsically make accessibility an operational transition. [Kripke, *Semantical Analysis of Modal Logic I* (1963), §2, p. 68; primary article scan](https://www.filosoficas.unam.mx/~morado/Cursos/17Modal/Kripke1963I.pdf), [publication record](https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.19630090502).

Effect4's [World record](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/World.lean:52) adds declaration tables Γ/Π/Ρ/Θ and a fixed service typing to machine ids/stores. [World.le](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/World.lean:132) requires allocation growth, preservation of old declarations, cell-typing compatibility and an unchanged service table. [leHost](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Validity.lean:36) additionally preserves external spellings at their indices. Its [reflexivity/transitivity and hostOrder](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Validity.lean:116) are existing theorems/definitions.

Crucially, these orders do not require global validity or source reachability. They describe admissible compatible worlds, including arbitrary invalid ones. Saying “later world” therefore means **accessible by this preorder**, not “the next machine state” or “a state this program will eventually reach.” A runtime preservation theorem must separately show that an actual covered transition admits an appropriate world extension and retains its state invariant. Accessibility alone establishes neither progress nor liveness.

## 2. Unary Kripke membership is legitimate; binary equivalence is additional structure

Ahmed explicitly defines **unary** Kripke logical relations as type/world-indexed predicates with monotonicity under accessibility (thesis §2.2.5, pp. 33–35). The value-transport map need not change the value; Effect4's membership transport keeps `v` fixed. This supports the term “Kripke logical predicate,” without requiring a binary relation. [Ahmed, *Semantics of Types for Mutable State* (2004), §2.2.5](https://www.ccs.neu.edu/home/amal/ahmedsthesis.pdf); [read text, lines 1755–1774](/Users/pooks/Dev/lean4-effect4/docs/research/2026-10-01-semantics/sources/text/ahmed-2004-semantics-of-types-for-mutable-state.txt:1755).

[ Fits : World → Val → Ty → Prop ](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Membership.lean:112) is such a unary membership predicate; [fits_mono](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Membership.lean:884) supplies its persistence under `leHost`. Reference membership checks declaration/type agreement, not the referent's contents recursively: [RefDeclared/PromiseDeclared](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Membership.lean:34); stored-content conditions have separate owners.

Ahmed–Dreyer–Rossberg instead interpret types by tuples `(k, W, v₁, v₂)` and relate two stores satisfying a world, then prove a connection to contextual equivalence (§§3.2–3.5, 4). That is a stronger, different purpose, not a theorem inherited by our unary predicate. [*State-Dependent Representation Independence* (POPL 2009), pp. 4–6](https://www.ccs.neu.edu/home/amal/papers/sdri.pdf); [read text](/Users/pooks/Dev/lean4-effect4/docs/research/2026-10-01-semantics/sources/text/ahmed-dreyer-rossberg-2009-state-dependent-representation-independence.txt:308). A binary relation, context/observation definition and corresponding soundness theorem would be needed for that claim.

## 3. Future-world continuations are a real adaptation

[TypedProg.store/fiber](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Residual.lean:266) choose a certificate, require permission now, and require the continuation to be typed for **every permitted answer at every accessible world**. Its [world-monotonicity theorem](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Residual.lean:728) uses transitivity to transport these clauses. [Iterator/loop protocols](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Residual.lean:388) and [async finalization](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Laws/Program/Typed/Residual.lean:425) similarly retain their contract under later worlds. `unguard`/`finishFinalizer` instead check a payload and impose no continuation premise; do not describe every constructor uniformly.

The closest effect-protocol source is de Vilhena–Pottier: protocol interpretation requires a permitted request and preparedness for all permitted replies (§3.3, pp. 33:9–10). Effect4 adapts this interaction discipline to plain propositions, explicit certificates and world persistence. Hazel additionally has separating resources, masks, logical updates and an execution-defined weakest precondition (§4.1). Its protocol “upward closure” (§3.4) is covariance in the reply postcondition, **not** our world-extension relation. [*A Separation Logic for Effect Handlers* (2021), §§3.3–4.1](https://cambium.inria.fr/~fpottier/publis/de-vilhena-pottier-sleh.pdf); [protocol interpretation](/Users/pooks/Dev/lean4-effect4/docs/research/2026-10-01-semantics/sources/text/de-vilhena-pottier-2021-separation-logic-for-effect-handlers.txt:463).

## 4. No arrow removes one difficulty, not every possible need for step indexing

Ahmed identifies a semantic type/store-typing circularity (§3.2.3, p. 43); stratification and lower indices make the definitions well founded (§3.3.5, Definition 3.5, pp. 54–55). Ahmed–Dreyer–Rossberg explain the analogous world/store-relation circularity for higher-order references (§3.4–3.5, pp. 4–5). Step indices stratify semantic dependencies; they are not simply an execution fuel counter.

At this snapshot, [Ty's complete signature](/Users/pooks/.codex/worktrees/metaprogramming-audit/lean4-effect4/src/Effect4/Program/Ty.lean:37) contains neither arrow nor recursive-type constructors; worlds store syntactic types rather than world-indexed semantic relations. `Fits` recurses over finite type structure and ends reference clauses in table lookups. These concrete choices explain why its current definition needs no step index. The slogan “no arrows, therefore no step indexing” is too strong: recursive types, impredicative invariants or richer world-dependent interpretations could introduce circularity separately. Iris demonstrates the invariant case and distinguishes its later modality from ordinary world extension (§5, p. 7; §6, p. 8). [Jung et al., *Iris* (2015)](https://iris-project.org/pdfs/2015-popl-iris1-final.pdf).

Lean functions in proof-side `RProgram` continuations are not stored arrow values and do not, by themselves, force such a circular semantic domain.

## 5. What modal structure is actually present?

**Mathematical inference:** on world predicates define `□P(w) := ∀w', w.leHost w' → P(w')`. The proved preorder supports the usual reflexive/transitive box reasoning. For an upward-closed predicate, `P ↔ □P`; `fits_mono` establishes that persistence for fixed value/type. Continuation clauses have this universal-future shape. This is a legitimate modal interpretation of their closure, not an existing declaration of a complete modal calculus, formula interpretation or S4 completeness theorem. World extension is also not Iris's guarded “later,” persistence/resource modality, or eventuality.

Simpson–Voorneveld's effect modalities instead lift **value predicates to computation-tree predicates**, using an interpretation `O → P(T1)` and transformed return leaves (§3, Definition 3.1, pp. 13–14). Their behavioural-equivalence/congruence results require additional conditions such as Scott openness and decomposability (§4). Effect4's world persistence supplies neither that tree interpretation nor those hypotheses. `TypedProg` has a computation-predicate shape, but identifying it with their modality requires an explicit construction and connecting law. [*Behavioural Equivalence via Modalities for Algebraic Effects*, arXiv v3 (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.08843).

## Recommended wording and work consequence

> Effect4 uses world-indexed unary semantic membership and Kripke-closed effect continuations. Accessibility means compatible extension of declarations, allocations and their stated invariants, not runtime reachability. Membership persists along this order; continuations must accept every protocol-permitted reply at every accessible world. This adapts possible-worlds typing and effect-protocol reasoning to the current first-order value language. It does not establish contextual equivalence, a step-indexed higher-order-store model, an Iris weakest precondition, or an effect-modal behavioural logic.

For implementation, the useful contract is: source admission → residual typing under exact protocol premises → actual-handler fulfillment/world extension → maintained machine invariant. Name each connector separately. Revisit semantic-domain construction when adding stored functions, recursive types or impredicative invariants; do not import step indexing merely because a world preorder exists. Introduce a new modal theory only when a specific observation/property requires its interpretation and laws.
