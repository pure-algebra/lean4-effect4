## What is proved

**The loop with its residue.** `driveStep` is one non-stuck command of `drive`, arm for arm.
`driveState` is `drive` returning the commands it did not run: fuel `0` keeps every command,
no command leaves nothing, a stuck machine keeps its commands. `drive_eq_driveState` says
`drive` is its first component. The splitting law

```
driveState_add : driveState interp (a + b) m c
               = driveState interp b (driveState interp a m c).1 (driveState interp a m c).2
drive_add      : drive interp (a + b) m c
               = drive interp b (driveState interp a m c).1 (driveState interp a m c).2
```

holds with no side condition; the corner cases compose because of the three facts above. From
it, `drive_stable_of_done : (driveState interp n m c).2 = [] → ∀ k, drive interp (n + k) m c
= drive interp n m c`, `drive_stable_of_stuck` the same when the loop halted, and
`drive_stable_of_settled` for either (`settled r := r.2.isEmpty || r.1.stuck.isSome`, since a
halted loop keeps its commands and exhaustion alone would call it insufficient).

**The trace only grows.** `RunMachine.emit` is the one writer of `trace`
(`Fibers.lean:456`). `RunMachine.Extends m m' := m.trace <+: m'.trace` is carried through
every helper `drive` reaches — `spawn`, `start`, `interruptEach`, `countdownPark`,
`linkScope`, `launchEntrant`, `injectYield`, `fireObserver` and its fold, `exitFiber`,
`finishFrame`, `stepFrame`, `finalizerOr`, `interruptThenJoin`, `withFiber`, `evaluatePrim`,
`iteration`, `settle`, `driveStep` — and then through `drive`, `stepDecision.fire`,
`stepDecision.flushAll`, `stepDecision.flushRoot`, `stepDecision` and `replayEval` (on the
machine inside its result). Each has the review's form as a corollary:
`drive_trace_extends : ∃ ev, (drive interp n m c).trace = m.trace ++ ev`, and the same for
`fire`, `flushAll`, `flushRoot`, `stepDecision`, `replayEval`. No refusal was needed: no interp
hook returns a machine, the hooks return stores.

The proofs are one tactic, `trace_chain with <hops>`: unfold the helper, zeta-reduce so every
`let (m, …) := helper …` becomes a projection, split every `if` and `match`, and at each
leaf compute the trace down to the machine the leaf was built from, then hop through that
helper's lemma and go on. The hop list grows down the file so that every name it mentions
exists where it is used; an `exact` on a name that does not exist yet does not fail, it
closes the goal with `sorry` and logs an error, which is why the list is staged.

**Fuel is monotone on one loop.** `drive_trace_mono : n ≤ n' → Extends (drive interp n m c)
(drive interp n' m c)`, the splitting law and the growth of the trace together.
`stepDecision_trace_mono` lifts it to the decisions that run one loop or none
(`SingleLoop`: `evaluate`, `answerAsync`, `interruptFrom`, `yieldVerdict`,
`installMiddleware`).

**The order and the three laws over `replayEval`.** `ReplayResult.le`: a `frontier m` is
below every result whose trace extends `m.trace`; `finished` and `stuck` are below themselves
only. `le_refl`, `le_trans`, `le_antisymm_terminal` (two frontiers with the same trace may
differ elsewhere, so the order is a preorder on frontiers, as the archived module's was up to
`observe`). The receipts `fireState`, `flushAllState`, `flushRootState`,
`stepDecisionState` mirror `fire`, `flushAll`, `flushRoot`, `stepDecision` with a flag —
every loop settled, every flush stopped with nothing armed (or a halt) before its rounds ran
out — each with its `_eq_` theorem and its `_stable` theorem (the flush ones in both the fuel
and the rounds). `Suffices interp fuel tape m : Bool` is the replay itself with the receipts,
decidable by construction. Then:

* compatibility, `replay_stable : Suffices interp n tape m = true → ∀ k, replayEval interp
  (n + k) tape m = replayEval interp n tape m`, and `Suffices_mono`;
* monotonicity, `replay_obs_mono_of_suffices` (where it is stability), and on a
  one-decision single-loop tape the frontier half `replay_frontier_mono_single` and the stuck
  half `replay_stuck_mono_single`;
* coherence, `leastSufficient interp tape m bound` by a hand-written bounded search
  (`leastUpTo`, no `Nat.find` here), with `leastSufficient_sound`, `_least`, `_le`,
  `_bound_mono`, `_isSome`, `replay_colimit` (the replay at the found fuel is the replay at
  every fuel above it) and `replay_colimit_eq_of_sufficient` (any sufficient fuel under the
  bound finds the same fuel and the same result: the colimit is a function of the tape and
  the machine, not of the bound or of the fuel that found it).

## What is refused, by name

* `APPROX-FB-REFRESH` — fuel is not monotone across a fuel refresh. `fire` gives every task
  the full fuel, `flushAll` and `flushRoot` every round, `replayEval` every decision, and a
  loop cut short is followed by the next unit of work on the half-done machine. Two
  executable refutations: `E4-APPROX-CE-003`, `pBindSync` under `[evaluate, interruptFrom
  none ∅ root]` at fuel `1` and `2` (traces `[started, interruptRecorded,
  interruptDeferred]` and `[started, frame, interruptRecorded, interruptDeferred]`, neither a
  prefix of the other); `E4-APPROX-CE-004`, two `start` tasks on the root's dispatcher fired
  at fuel `1` and `2` (14 and 16 events, not prefixes). The review's `replay_obs_mono` over
  an arbitrary tape is therefore false on this machine; it is stated on one loop, and along
  a tape under `Suffices`. The one repair that would make it true everywhere is a sticky
  frontier in `drive` (a marker, as the Flow runner had), which changes the machine and is
  the owner's call, not this lane's.
* `APPROX-FB-FINISHED` — `ReplayResult.finished` at an insufficient fuel is not proved
  terminal. Every fiber has exited, but a residual `Cmd.drainDue` may change the store
  through `interp.dueResumes`, which the interp chooses. On the `Api` interp the drained store
  is the same store when nothing is due, so no executable refutation of "finished stays
  finished" was found; what is refuted is "finished means the fuel sufficed"
  (`E4-APPROX-CE-002`: `pSucceed` at fuel `3` is `finished` with `[drainDue, drainDue]`
  unrun; `pYieldNow` under the ordinary tape is `finished` from `5` and sufficient from `7`;
  the two-child program is `finished` from `16` and sufficient from `20`). `replay_stable`
  carries `Suffices`, not the outcome.
* Not here: the `fuelFor` allotment for `Eff` programs; any statement about rc.112; anything
  called a bisimulation.

## For the budget lane

The other seat proves that a run whose op count reaches `defaultBudget` parks on a yield, is
fired by `flush`, and still reaches its meaning. What it can reuse from here, stated exactly:

* `drive_add interp a b m cmds : drive interp (a + b) m cmds = drive interp b (driveState
  interp a m cmds).1 (driveState interp a m cmds).2` — to cut a long loop at the yield and
  continue with what was left;
* `drive_stable_of_done interp n m cmds (h : (driveState interp n m cmds).2 = []) : ∀ k,
  drive interp (n + k) m cmds = drive interp n m cmds` — to trade the contract's fixed fuel
  for "enough";
* `fire_stable`, `flushAll_stable interp fuel rounds m (h : (flushAllState interp fuel rounds
  m).2 = true) : ∀ k j, flushAll interp (fuel + k) (rounds + j) m = flushAll interp fuel
  rounds m` — the rounds of `flush` once the last dispatcher is quiet;
* `drive_trace_extends`, `stepDecision_trace_extends` — the trace only grows, should the
  budget lane's row want the `yieldInjected` event to survive the rest of the run.

Nothing here reads `defaultBudget`, `Api.run`'s tape, or `Program`; the lane is closed on
the machine alone.
