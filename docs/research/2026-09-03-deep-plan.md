# The deep plan, v2: one reference machine, built design-first, landed once

Status: final, 2026-09-03. Supersedes v1 of this file and waves 2 to 5 of
`docs/research/2026-09-03-refactor-plan.md` as a schedule; keeps that plan's nine rules for
the landing. Evidence: the four research notes (`2026-09-03-deep-{fiber-runloop,
state-models,flow-to-frames,gates-and-surfaces}.md`), the adversarial review
(`2026-09-03-deep-plan-review.md`, eighteen findings, all folded in below), and the fiber
machine's Pass A contract (`2026-09-03-fiber-machine-pass-a.md`). The concrete spelling of
the carriers is `workshop/deep/Fibers.lean`, which elaborates against the built library.

Coverage numbers in this file are static reads of the coverage module and are never quoted
in a handoff; the report script's block at a named commit is.

## 0. The rulings this version rests on

1. **Design first, land once.** The carriers are written under `workshop/` (outside every
   `lean_lib`, so the trust tokenizer and the module-closure gate do not see them), validated
   by executable witnesses, then proved, then moved into `Effect4/` with contract, battery,
   axiom report, register rows and census join in one landing. No breaker or gate runs
   before the landing. The refactor plan's nine rules apply at the landing.
2. **Reuse the work, not the carrier.** The frame machine (`Effect4/Runtime/Runtime.lean`),
   `Scope`, `ScopeMachine`, `ScopeRestoration`, `Supervision`'s fork and observer logic,
   `Scheduler`'s tape and replay shape, and the `RegionSimulation` compile are reused. The
   fiber is a **fresh record with one source of truth**; the old `FiberState` and
   `Scheduler.Machine` are *computed* from it (`RunFiber.toSup`, `RunMachine.toSched`),
   never stored beside it, so no coherence invariant exists and every old theorem transfers
   through a `rfl`-shaped projection lemma. Old proofs are not redone. `Race.lean` is left in
   place; retiring it is a ruling at landing, not a refactor (review finding 8).
3. **Fork is an operation, not a terminator** (review findings 1, 2, 14). Flow names a
   concurrent child through an operation of the supplied alphabet whose request names a
   declared root and carries `daemon : Bool` and `region : Option RegionId`, and whose answer
   is a fiber handle. No `RawTerm`/`RegionTerm` constructor changes, no admission clause, no
   region denotation summand, none of the 28 exhaustive matches in `Effect4/` nor the 9
   upstream, and no `effects` or `typescript` bump. The lowering emits a service call and
   never `Effect.fork`; that is a recorded refusal. A Flow v4 fork terminator that emits
   `Effect.fork` is a later packet, priced with the two package bumps it needs.
4. **`PrimInterp` stays pure; the machine has the state slot** (finding 4). `RunInterp`
   extends `PrimInterp` with the store interface (`syncState`, `registerAsync`,
   `dueResumes`, `dropFinalizer`); the state models are the components of `St`; the oracle
   form is a corollary through `statelessRun`. Under DB-07 the machine threads `St` out on
   the failure arm and never restores it. `armA`/`armE` are restated at `RunInterp` only when
   a finalizer or continuation touches a store.
5. **`Prim` gains the three park primitives** (`yieldNowWith`, `async`, `asyncFinalizer`),
   as the frames packet reserved (`Runtime.lean:90-92`). `Prim.cases_receipt` goes to
   seventeen, the fourteen exhaustive matches are extended, and the `popFrom` fusion agreement
   is proved or the loops unfused (`docs/FRAMES-DAG.md:200-211`). The frames contract is
   re-frozen once, at the landing (finding 5).
6. **`Prim` is a target-profile machine syntax with no admission of its own** (finding 12).
   The machine carries the invariant that every `Prim` it steps is the compile of an admitted
   Flow, as a theorem where the fork operation makes it provable and otherwise as a
   `WellSourced` predicate with a refusal row. `docs/DESIGN-BASIS.md` gains one paragraph
   under DB-02 saying so, at the landing.
7. **Decision kinds are partitioned, not unified** (finding 13, DB-03). The Flow tape owns
   `choose` and `branch`; the interrupt tape owns delivery at a Flow interrupt point;
   `RunDecision` owns dispatcher firing, the yield verdict, the external async answer, the
   machine-level interrupt with its interruptor id, and the middleware latch. One interrupt
   *model*, two decision kinds, with the separation proved in the `sitesSeparated` shape.
8. **The error channel is one carrier everywhere.** `Cause ε δ ι α` (fail, die, interrupt,
   annotations) and `Exit` flow through every park, resume, observer, join and store path:
   an async resumes with any primitive, including `Prim.failure`; a Deferred completes with an
   effect, so `fail` and `interruptWith` are `Prim.failure` completions; `join` continues with
   the child's exit as an effect and `await` with it as a value (`RunInterp.exitValue`); the
   interruptor id is carried in the cause. At the Flow level the error terms are `performCatch`
   with `errorTy`, whose compile is the `onSuccessAndFailure` repair (P3), and the lowering's
   `Result` reading. Nothing in the plan collapses typed failure, defect and interruption.

## 3. Order and parallelism

S1 (worktree), S2 and S3 run in parallel; they share no file. S4 follows S1 because P1 to P3
rewrite proofs that `cases` over `Prim`'s constructor list. S5 follows S2 and S3. L follows
all five. Three Lean processes at most; the machine is memory-bound.

## 4. The theorems, grouped

Statements, not proofs. Sources: Pass A §4 and §5; the fiber note R1/R2; the review.

- **Step equations**, one per rc.112 arm, cited by line in the docstring as the census rows
  are: the run-loop top, yield injection once per entry, park and resume, async short-circuit
  and park, fork deferred and immediate, children interrupted after the exit, observers in
  index order, interrupt recorded always and applied only when idle and interruptible,
  dispatcher drained once, buckets ascending and FIFO.
- **Invariants**: at most one exit; observers cleared exactly once; `running` restored;
  fresh ids; a parked fiber is not running; the five forbidden examples unreachable.
- **Projections**: `toSup (exitFiber r e) = ((toSup r).publish e).1` and its siblings;
  `toSched (step m d) = stepEval boundary (toSched m) (decisionOf d)` where defined; one
  fiber with no fork and no park equals `FrameFiber.run`.
- **Masks and delivery**, Marlow et al.'s shapes: an interrupt is applied only at an
  interruptible point; a masked fiber's pending cause is delivered at the unmask frame; a
  finalizer runs exactly once; the two interrupt decision kinds never name the same site.
- **Adequacy**, Abadi and Plotkin's shape: for a compatible tape the machine's service-level
  trace equals the traced service's under the existing masks; the machine's fuel bound is a
  sum over live fibers, stated in the contract.
- **State**, DB-07: the store after a failing step is the store the failing fiber had
  reached; the oracle form of every simulation theorem is a corollary of the stateful one.
- **Compile**: T5 and T6 on the full region surface under `stateless`; the fork operation's
  compile; `WellSourced` on every compiled program.
- **Stores**: the 31 Ref, Deferred and ScopeStore clauses of the state note §3, one theorem
  each; `deferred.await` as a store park, not an `op.Async`.

## 5. Decisions

Settled by the user's rulings above: design-first and one landing; fresh carrier with
computed projections; fork as an operation; the state slot on `RunInterp`; the three park
constructors and one frames re-freeze; `Race.lean` left in place until the landing ruling;
the decision partition; the error channel as one carrier.

Settled by the spikes (S1 and S3 reports): the operation route costs `Effect4/` one arm of
`compileRegion`, one field of `regionInterp` (`contE`, which is packet P3 for the profile
arm), and one appended `Rule` tag, against 28 plus 9 exhaustive matches and two package
bumps on the terminator route; the fused `popFrom` disagreed with rc.112 on every
successful async resume once an `AsyncFinalizer` exists, so the loop is unfused and the
divergence is a theorem; the decision partition is proved; `WellSourced` is proved for
every program-carrying action and its closure under the step is stated on the fiber's whole
configuration, not `current` alone.

Still to be taken at the landing, with recommendations:

0. A flow has one `resultTy` for every declared root (`Effects/Flow/Admission.lean:895-896`),
   so a forked child returns the parent's result type; per-root result types are a carrier
   change in the pinned `effects` package on either route. Recommended: record it as a
   refusal row now, and schedule the `effects` release only when a witness needs it.
   `RegionSimulation` fixes `ι := Unit`, so no compiled flow can carry an interruptor id
   until the landing instantiates `ι` at a fiber-id carrier; no `interrupt.*` clause is
   claimed over a compiled flow before that.

1. Re-dispose the ten `targetOnly` scheduler rows to `separateCalculus` (denominator 117 to
   127 before any turns green). Recommended: yes, with both report blocks pasted.
2. `LayerDesc.mergeAll` carries `List LayerId`. Recommended: yes.
3. The `Deferreds` family moves from the harness generator into the library. Recommended:
   yes.
4. `scope.close-sequential` stays partial until the machine's sequential close is the
   witness. Recommended: yes.
5. After the landing `Scheduler.lean` and `Supervision.lean` witness no `fork.*` or
   `interrupt.*` row and remain `separateCalculus` owners of a relational scheduler;
   projection lemmas are evidence, never a closure route. Recommended: yes.
6. The gate host is the WSL clone; the Windows checkout is for interactive work.
   Recommended: yes.

## 6. What this plan does not claim

The host is never the subject of a theorem; TRACE-DAG `bridges` cannot close by theorem. The
lowering emits a service call for a fork, never `Effect.fork`, and the compile never emits
`Prim.iterator`; both are refusal rows. No census row turns green from a golden, a probe or
a projection lemma; green is clause by clause against the census summary, and the only
sanctioned number is the report script's block at a named commit. The machine takes the
cross-dispatcher order from its tape; when the host would have fired it stays evidence.
