Effect4 Visual Semantics: The Tube Language Inventory

Every glyph of Effect4 drawn exclusively with the tube pen. The pipework constitutes the program invariants; the contents constitute the state. 37 marks over 7 physical kinds shown at 96px and 32px on Dark Ground (#14110d, ink #ece6dc) and Paper Ground (#f7f4ee, ink #1b1f2a).

Body Family (10 marks)

A compact closed vessel, fitting, or discrete payload. Ends are closed (rounded) denoting self-containment, except when mounted inline on a process conduit.

Contents: Carries the payload or evaluated state value. An empty lumen is an operational envelope; a filled lumen holds the discrete data payload.
Ends: Closed rounded ends (stroke-linecap: round) for independent bodies; square cut ends (butt) for continuous conduit interfaces.
Invariance: The central lumen axis and outer casing envelope remain invariant; morphs modulate internal fluid fill or boundary scale smoothly.

Vessel Family (6 marks)

A bistable containment chamber governed by promise and fulfillment. Always sealed with rounded closed heads.

Contents: Empty lumen indicates a promise owed (park token, task, finalizer); filled fluid pill indicates promise fulfilled.
Ends: Always rounded closed heads (stroke-linecap: round), signifying pressure-retaining chambers.
Invariance: The chamber envelope and axis remain static; fulfillment changes fluid fill from zero to 100% in place.
