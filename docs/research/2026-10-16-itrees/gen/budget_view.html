Budget frames

Each frame is the run of the budgeted call tree at budget k under one host: Lean's denoteRowsB k where it has an arm, with definitions unfolded k calls deep (BudgetDefs.v). Frames only grow; the first frame that is not cut is the program's end, and every later frame is the same (Api.frames_grow, BudgetDefs.finishesD_iff). A program that is cut at every budget diverges (BudgetDefs.divergesD_iff); the frames below show a finite number of budgets only.
