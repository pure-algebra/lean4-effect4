# Upstream backlog: findings against the pinned Effect runtime

Rows here are behaviours of the pinned `effect@4.0.0-rc.112` source (upstream commit
`2600f62f`) that contradict its own types or documentation. The machine records each intentional departure as a signed divergence; a row records the reproduction, the comparison that shows it is a
defect and not a design choice, what we did about it on our side, and whether it has been
reported. Reporting is the owner's decision. Earlier host findings recorded inside
design-issue rows (DI-24's non-nominal service keys, DI-39's `tagIs` type-guard
disagreements, DI-97's `Deferred.poll` answer) and in the research notes stay where they
are until someone touches them; new findings go here.
