p4 is CE-1 of ir/Counterexamples.lean: `Code.Machine` answers `uncaught 103` on the transform's
output, all three hosts answer 18.  p4_callback_trap.js.excerpt is the generated JavaScript
around the top-level trap: the `Pushtrap` became a JavaScript `try { … } catch` wrapping the
`caml_callback`, which is why the exception thrown out of `caml_callback` (jslib.js:100) is
still caught.

p5 and p6 differ only in a reference to `Effect.Unhandled`.  Without it `%perform` is the only
mention of `Stdlib.Effect`, and `%perform` is an external, so `Stdlib__Effect` is not linked,
its `Callback.register_exception` never runs, and `fiber.c:668-682` (cache_named_exception)
`fprintf`s "Fatal error: exception %s" and `exit(2)`s from C rather than raising.  With the
reference, all three hosts agree that `perform` at the root raises a catchable
`Effect.Unhandled` — which is CE-2.
