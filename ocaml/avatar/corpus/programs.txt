# The adversarial corpus. Round five of spike A0.
#
# One record per program, blank-line separated. See `corpus_dsl.ml` for the grammar.
# A program with no `tape` line gets 32 all-`false` entries, so a fork never hands the
# processor over unless the program says so; handovers are written as explicit `yield` steps
# or as an explicit tape.
#
# Body codes 0-6 are the standard table of `git:606918eb73daefcc235a261fce879bf910f2471e:harness/trace/fibers-tail.ts` (0 succeeds 11,
# 1 succeeds 22, 2 fails 1, 3 fails 2, 4 never, 5 masks across a yield, 6 completes the
# Deferred at handle 0). Codes 10 and up are declared per program.
